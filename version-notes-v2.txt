Version 2. The empirical scale Θ_c = (2πΦ(a)/(2πc − 9/2))⁴ has been replaced by the Fuchs scale d_c = (2¹⁴/3)√2 π⁵ c^{9/2} e^{−4πc}, that is, by the asymptotics of the defect of the prolate spheroidal function with index 4. The ratio Θ_c/d_c grows as √c, while the upper bound on λ_min(Q_c) for large c in the work of Andrews, Connes and La Gatta (arXiv:2611.13129, Theorem 3.1) is of order d_c (ln c)³. Therefore the numerical remark of version 1 that for c ≤ 47 the quantity λ_min(Q_c) behaves as c⁵e^{−4πc} has been replaced by the statement that these data do not determine the power of c. Theorem 1.1(b) is written in the form ε_c ≥ 3.4 d_c, and the ratios in the tables have been recomputed in units of d_c. The proofs, certificates and the constants ε_c are unchanged. In addition, the formulations of several statements and proofs have been refined.
